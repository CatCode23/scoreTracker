# Scorer Quick Start (phone)

**Site:** `https://catcode23.github.io/scoreTracker/`

## Sign in (once per device)

1. Tap **Staff sign in**.
2. Enter your email and the password the operations cell gave you, then tap **Sign in**.
3. The first time, open the menu (☰), tap **Change Password**, and set your own password
   (at least 10 characters).
4. If you see "Account pending activation", give your email to the operations cell.
5. Forgot your password? Ask the operations cell to reset it.

You stay signed in on that phone, so avoid tapping **Sign out**.

> A QR code only opens the site. It never signs you in.

## Enter a result

**1. Select match. 2. Enter result. 3. Save.**

1. Open the menu (☰) and tap **Matches**.
2. Pick your **event**, then tap the **match** (the **To play** tab lists what's left).
   You only see matches at your assigned venue.
3. Enter the result for each team. Team names are read-only.
   - **Games:** the score for each team, then tap the **winner**.
   - **Heats:** the time (`12:34.5` or seconds), any penalty seconds, and the place.
   - **Stations:** the place or technical score.
4. Tap **Save Official Result**. It is live on the public site straight away.

If your account is a **Scorer** account, the button says **Submit Result** instead and the
result shows **Awaiting Approval** until an event administrator approves it.

## Made a mistake?

Event administrators: open **Matches**, tap the match (**Finished** tab) and tap **Edit result**.
Correct it, give a short reason, and save.

Scorer accounts: tell an event administrator; they will fix it.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Awaiting Approval | From a Scorer account, waiting for an administrator |
| Official | Public |
| Returned for Correction | Enter it again |
| Disputed | Being reviewed by the sport manager |
| Void | Not counted |

## No signal?

Tap **Sync / Refresh** first.

**If connectivity is unavailable, complete the venue paper scorecard and report to the
designated sport manager or operations cell.**

## Do not

- Do not enter names of individuals, medical information, or anything sensitive in the
  notes.
- Do not share your password or let someone else use your account.
